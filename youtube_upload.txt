MoveMate AI is a free, browser-based AI personal trainer I built
for the Walter Payton College Prep Coding Club Health and Fitness
Technology Hackathon 2026.

Most teenagers who want to get fit face the same problem:
personal trainers cost $50–$150 per session, generic workout apps
give everyone the same plan, and nobody is watching your form
when you work out alone at home. Bad form leads to injury and
most people quit within weeks.

MoveMate AI solves all of this using just your laptop webcam and
artificial intelligence — completely free, no download required.

WHAT IT DOES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ Generates a personalised multi-week workout plan based on
   your age, weight, height, fitness goal and timeline

✅ Uses Google MediaPipe AI to track 33 points on your body
   through your webcam at 30 frames per second

✅ Draws a real-time skeleton overlay so you can see every
   joint being tracked

✅ Automatically counts your reps using real joint angle
   mathematics — squats only count if your knee goes below
   105 degrees and back above 160 degrees

✅ Gives live form feedback on screen — "Keep chest up",
   "Go lower", "Keep body straight"

✅ Scores your form out of 100% updated in real time

✅ Tracks calories burned, workout streaks, and progress
   over time

✅ All AI runs 100% in the browser — your webcam video is
   never uploaded anywhere (fully private)

  STEP BY STEP — HOW TO UPLOAD
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Go to https://youtube.com
2. Click your profile picture (top right)
3. Click "YouTube Studio"
4. Click the "CREATE" button (top right, camera icon)
5. Click "Upload video"
6. Drag your video file in or click "SELECT FILES"
7. While it uploads, fill in:
     - Title: copy from the TITLE section above
     - Description: copy from the DESCRIPTION section above
     - Thumbnail: upload your screenshot with skeleton overlay
     - Tags: copy from the TAGS section above
     - Category: Science & Technology
     - Language: English
8. Click "NEXT" through the details screens
9. On "Visibility" — select PUBLIC
10. Click "PUBLISH"
11. Copy the video URL from the address bar
    It will look like: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXXXXXXXXXX
12. Paste that URL into your hackathon submission form
