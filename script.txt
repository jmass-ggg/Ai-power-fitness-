SCENE 1 — INTRODUCTION (face to camera, 20 seconds)
==============================================================

[Look directly at the camera. Speak naturally.]

"Hey everyone. My name is [YOUR NAME], and this is MoveMate AI —
a free, browser-based AI personal trainer that I built for this
hackathon.

The idea is simple: not everyone can afford a gym or a personal
trainer. But almost everyone has a laptop and a webcam.
So I built an app that uses AI to watch you work out, count
your reps, correct your form in real time, and give you a
personalised fitness plan — completely free, right in your browser."

[Pause 1 second, then turn to your screen.]

"Let me show you how it works."

==============================================================
SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM (screen showing the landing page, 20 seconds)
==============================================================

[Show the landing/home page of the app on screen]

"Here is the problem I am solving.

Most teenagers who want to get fit face three things:
one — they cannot afford a personal trainer.
two — generic workout apps give everyone the same plan.
three — nobody is watching you work out at home, so you
could be doing push-ups with terrible form and not even know it.

That is exactly what MoveMate AI fixes."

==============================================================
SCENE 3 — SIGN UP AND PROFILE (screen recording, 30 seconds)
==============================================================

[Open the app at localhost:5173 or your hosted URL]
[Click Register and sign up with a test account]

"First, you create a free account — just email and password,
no credit card, nothing like that."

[After registering, go to the profile/goal page]

"Then you fill in your details — your age, height, current weight,
your activity level, and what you want to achieve.

In my case I am going to say I want to lose weight over the
next eight weeks."

[Fill in the form on screen]

"The AI uses this information — your body mass, your goal,
and your timeline — to calculate exactly how many calories
you need to burn each day. This is personalised to YOU,
not a generic plan copied from a website."

==============================================================
SCENE 4 — AI PLAN GENERATION (screen recording, 20 seconds)
==============================================================

[Click Generate Plan or the equivalent button]

"Now I click Generate Plan.

The backend AI builds a complete week-by-week workout roadmap
for me. Every single day has specific exercises, sets, reps,
and a calorie target — all calculated based on my numbers."

[Show the roadmap or plan overview on screen]

"I can see my full plan right here. Today is Day 1.
Let me open today's workout."

==============================================================
SCENE 5 — DASHBOARD (screen recording, 15 seconds)
==============================================================

[Show the dashboard]

"The dashboard shows everything I need —
today's exercises, my calorie burn this week,

Everything updates automatically after each workout."

==============================================================
SCENE 6 — STARTING THE AI WORKOUT (screen recording, 20 seconds)
==============================================================

[Click on today's workout, then click Start Workout]
[Show the exercise selection screen]

"Now here is the part that makes MoveMate AI different from
every other fitness app.

I pick my exercise — let's go with Squats —
and I click Start AI Webcam Workout."

[Click the button]

"The browser asks for camera permission. I allow it.
The AI model loads — this is Google MediaPipe, a pose
detection model that runs one hundred percent inside
the browser. My webcam video never gets sent anywhere."

==============================================================
SCENE 7 — THE AI IN ACTION (screen recording with webcam, 60 seconds)
==============================================================

[This is the most important part — make sure lighting is good
and your full body is visible in the frame]

[The green skeleton overlay should appear on your body]

"And there it is — you can see the green skeleton overlay
on my body. The AI is tracking 33 points on my body right now —
my knees, my hips, my shoulders — all in real time at
30 frames per second."

[Point to the stats on screen]

"Up here I can see my rep counter, which set I am on,
my form score as a percentage, and my calories burned.
Down at the bottom is the live feedback telling me
exactly what to fix."

[Now do a squat — go all the way down and come back up]

"Watch what happens when I do a squat."

[Do the squat — the rep counter should go to 1]

"One rep counted. The AI measured my knee angle —
it only counts a rep if I go below 105 degrees
and come back above 160 degrees. Half reps do not count.
This is real exercise science, not just guessing."

[Do another squat but stay a bit shallow — intentionally bad form]

"Now watch what happens if my form is not great —
you can see the feedback changes immediately.
It says 'Go lower' or 'Keep chest up' — 
just like a real trainer would tell you."

[Do 2 or 3 more good squats]

"And the form score updates in real time. 
When I do it correctly, the score stays high —
when my form slips, it drops and I get a warning."

==============================================================
FILMING TIPS
==============================================================

FOR THE AI WORKOUT SCENE (Scene 7):
  - Make sure the room is well-lit — natural light is best
  - Step back from the camera until your full body fits the frame
  - For squats: face the camera directly
  - For push-ups: use a side view (camera to your left or right)
  - Wear clothes that contrast with your background
    (dark clothes on light wall, or light clothes on dark wall)
  - The green skeleton should appear clearly on your body

FOR SCREEN RECORDING:
  - Use OBS Studio (free) or your system's built-in recorder
  - On Windows: Win + G to open Game Bar recorder
  - On Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5
  - Record at 1080p if possible

FOR AUDIO:
  - Use headphones to avoid echo
  - Record in a quiet room
  - Speak at a normal pace — do not rush
